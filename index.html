Process / 01–06

How a project
actually runs here.

This is also how every case study on this site is written. It's the same order every time.

01

The problem

What's actually broken, slow, confusing, or costing the business money. I start there, not with a mood board.

02

The thinking

Competitors, constraints, and what's already been tried. What I rejected goes in the notes too, so the decision is documented, not vibes.

03

The business

Every choice has to survive contact with reality: pricing, timeline, team size, and whatever technical debt already exists.

04

The users

What people are actually trying to get done, and where they get stuck doing it. Observation over assumption.

05

The build

Brand, interface, and working software, built by the same hands, so nothing gets lost between a deck and a repository.

06

The outcome

What changed after it shipped. If I don't have a number, I say so instead of inventing one.
